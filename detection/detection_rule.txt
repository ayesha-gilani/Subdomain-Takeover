RULE NAME:
Possible Subdomain Takeover Exposure

DESCRIPTION:
Detects log patterns consistent with a potentially dangling
subdomain pointing to an external hosting provider.

DETECTION CONDITIONS:
1. DNS record is a CNAME.
2. CNAME target points to an external/third-party provider.
3. A subsequent HTTP response indicates that the expected
   resource is unavailable.

SUSPICIOUS INDICATORS:
- HTTP status: 404
- "No such application"
- "Not Found"
- "Unknown host"
- "Resource does not exist"

ALERT:
Generate a "Possible Subdomain Takeover Exposure" alert
when an external CNAME is followed by an unavailable-resource
indicator.

SEVERITY:
Medium — potential exposure requiring analyst validation.

FALSE POSITIVE CONSIDERATIONS:
A temporary service outage or incorrectly configured third-party
resource may produce similar indicators. The alert must therefore
be validated against DNS ownership, asset inventory, and the
responsible application/team before escalation.
